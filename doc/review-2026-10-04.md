# Targeted functional review — 2026-10-04

## Scope

Shared scene state, timer, sample invalidation, state application/reset and existing wave-equation/preset suites.

## Finding and fix

### Paused field sampling

The sampling optimization watched physics properties but did not watch the public timer property. Changing the clock while paused left field buffers at the old time even after step(0). Clock changes now mark the buffers dirty. A regression compares the paused result with an explicitly sampled reference at the requested time.
